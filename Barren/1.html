Day 1

A bad dream where I can't see anything. Each way I look - nothing. Can't tell you what it is. Darkness finds itself everywhere. My head spins for a short while, not used to it. I feel something soft under. A bed? Probably. I move my hand around, it doesn't meet anything though. This tells me something, I am somewhere empty. I can feel the… bed? Under. I sit up and try to get used to the black. No use. Getting up, I feel the cold, wet ground. It's cold. The place reeks of staleness and dust. I wonder, am I outside? Where exactly? I take a few steps forward… And trip over the same bed-like structure. It's as dark as everything else. Wonder if there's ever something beyond nothing. … Time is ticking. Time is ticking, and yet the stillness is the same. Just how much time passed? I give up. No entrance, no exit, not anything! Nothing! Nothing! Nothing! I lean back against this bed-like structure. It's soft. Home… Wish I was home right now. Working the most basic job is so much better than this! Tears begin falling down my cheeks. Maybe if I make myself smaller, I will go back. Why is this room cold? Why is it so empty? Why is there no exit? How do I wake up? How? Can I even wake up? What?

Day 2

I wake up again.

…

Same spot, cold and wet. I try to look at my hands and meet nothing. They land on my face. Dread fills me. Not just dread, but powerlessness too. There's rustling in the distance. There wasn't any rustling yesterday. It goes like - rattle, rattle, boom boom, rattle. The longer I hear it, the more it piques my interest. Is it someone or something? Morse code? Rattle, Rattle, Rattle…

When will it stop? That rattling has become stale. Everything else is already stale. Boredom really feels weird, I hit my hand against this bed-like structure a few times. God, the rattling won't stop or something! I get up and try to find where it comes from. Thankfully it stops… Thankfully? What am I talking about? That's terrible, now I can't even guess where it was coming from. Looking in each direction… Nothing? Something. First thing I have noticed for the past however long. A tiny, dim reflection. Coming closer - it is something I can touch. After failing a few times, the door opens.

It's another room. Stepping inside, I see things. It isn't as dark as the last one. Seems to be an apartment of sorts. From where I stand, on the left finds itself a kitchen counter. On the right? A couch. Someone's sitting on it and watching the dim TV. What a weird someone, they have these bumps on their head. I slowly walk over to them and tap their shoulder.

The figure turns around to look at me. Their eyes widen. In that moment, a scream leaves their lips. I crouch and cover my ears. It is too loud. I look up and see the figure staring at me from above. Their face is barely readable.

„Who the hell are you?” - they asked. I drop.

Day 3

An unfamiliar ceiling. At least I can see a ceiling now. I feel something spiky press against my neck, turning to the side I see this person from yesterday. They're uncomfortably close to me. Noticing I'm awake they part their lips, then close them and exhale loudly. Even the air out of their nose is cold. Bumps on top of their head twitch too. What are those?

„Who are you?”

I don't answer. Busy looking at something wagging behind them. „Hello? Are you there? Cat got your tongue?” - They keep asking, adding a soft „Maybe” after. Finally, I say „W-What are those bumps on your head? And the thing? Wagging, I mean, behind you?” The person blinks. Then they blink again. And again. „This?” They point to their head, „ … Those are my ears - have you never seen ears before?” At that point I questioned what ears are. The person stands up and turns around… Revealing a tail. I sit up. Shock fills my body. A human mixed in with some kind of an animal? It can't be true, right? I instinctively reach out my hand to touch the tail.

The person turns back around to face me. „I haven't introduced myself - I'm Oliver, though people call me Ollie.” I have never met a person named Ollie. Are we even on the same planet? „Ollie… What day is it?” - I ask. Silence. Ollie looks questioningly at me. Sighs, then points to a blank paper in the kitchen. I get up and walk over to look at it. It is a calendar that shows March 4th…? Wasn't it September a while ago? It doesn't say what year it is, just March 4th.

March 5th

I'm taking a stroll outside. It's colder than Ollie's house. I feel like as though there's only evening. Time doesn't move. One way to describe it - barren.

People outside are grim too. Pairs clutch themselves close, lonely people walk far away from others. It's completely different than what I'm used to. The street is empty of any cars, but full of buildings. I pass by a pawnbroker, a bank, a supermarket, all devoid of any normalcy. I keep walking. Then stop and go into the centre of the city.

The centre reminds me of where I used to live. A giant square in the middle with trees around it. „I see trees of green, red roses too…” is what Louis Armstrong would've said… Not here. Here? The trees barely have any colour. I also have not seen a single rose. Is there something that isn't bleak in this world? Seeing a bench, I decide to sit and watch people walk by. Some of them look at me, like I looked weird. Some of them didn't even see me. One came up and gave me a banknote, then said „You probably need it more than I do”. Looking at it, the banknote had a huge 10 written on it. What a kind fellow.

Bonus chapter: Tenderness

I'm watching the television. So far, the only interesting thing was an advertisement for something. I don't understand those things. So much for entertainment, I guess. While watching, I hear Niko prepare pans in the kitchen. Wonder what he'll cook today. Quiet hitting of them fills the apartment. Footsteps, Ollie's footsteps. I turn around to look into the kitchen and see… love.

Love. What is love? (baby don't hurt me)

Ollie holds Niko. Niko holds pans. I stare. Feels weird to stare at such a moment, but I can't help it. Niko finishes taking out the pans and turns around to face Ollie. They hug, get close, stare at each other's eyes. Niko's apron dangles. Ollie picks him up into the air. I have never seen something like that, not in the Barren at least. I turn back around to look at the television. The sound doesn't escape me, though. The way they are close and personal is loud.

I can feel burning on my face. It's embarrassing. It's so embarrassing. The television is too quiet. Behind me are two love birds, two love birds that just want to make me feel embarrassed! A soft thud. I quickly look behind, it's Niko's apron. My heart skips a beat… They're kissing! I give up on everything and look back on the television. It brings no luck, as the current show is also about love. Just yesterday there was no love in this city!

Something pokes my neck. I turn back around like a rusty screw. Slowly, stiffly, red and with wide eyes.

„Enjoyed what you saw?” - Niko asks. His smirk will stay in my nightmares forever. This all knowing smile will haunt me. „I- I… No! Go away!” I stammer. It's not true though. I lied. I enjoyed it. Niko doesn't give up. „You're like a vegetable. Actually, a vegetable has less colours than you… of course they do” Then he looks away and smiles. I try to think of a response, but he is already gone. Gone, giggling with Ollie. If only I could disappear. I look at the television (again).

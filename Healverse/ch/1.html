Prologue

BACK

Me and Cast Heal Ball sit on the couch in the living room.

It is our favourite spot, because it has the best view of the TV. The TV is buzzing about news, but nobody is watching that.

Cast Heal Ball looks at me, spins and finds the courage to ask about food.

We're both broke. That apparently doesn't stop Cast Heal Ball from asking. I sigh. Not the first time, and probably won't be the last.

I look directly at Cast Heal Ball and say, "We're dead broke. You can look in the fridge if you want some food." Cast Heal Ball spins again and says, "I know a job."

"A job? Last time you said that, we ended up in damageverse. I am not risking my life for some pizza again."

Cast Heal Ball spins a few times. "This job is different. We can get a load of dough without dragging ourselves into damageverse."

"Your jobs suck. How much dough are we arguing about, anyway?" I ask.

"Enough to get us a load of food."

I let out an exasperated groan and drag my hands down my face. This ball is going to be the death of us both. It's insane.

"Who's got the job?" I ask.

Cast Heal Ball spins and answers, "I don't know, man, I'm just a spinning ball."

Of course you don't; you're like a freeloader with quests that would end everyone who tried them...

"So what do we do?" I look at Cast Heal Ball. "You got me hungry, and I'm not ready to die on the couch."

Cast Heal Ball thinks. "Let me see what's in the fridge." The ball goes into the kitchen.

Minutes pass, and I hear some rustling. Cast Heal Ball comes back with leftover soup from yesterday. The ball looks at me, spins, and grins. "I got some food, and you don't, loser."

Damn ball. I get up and slowly make my way over to the kitchen.

Walking through the door, I see complete chaos. Fridge open, drawers are out. Nothing is in its original place. Did a tornado hit this kitchen?

"Hey, ball! What in the WORLD is all this?" I yell. Cast Heal Ball pauses before answering. "Nothin'. I forgot to clean up."

Wha- Who- Why? Huh? How do you even end up with this mess? Where do I even start?

Whatever. I walk out of the kitchen, sit back on the couch, and grab the bowl of soup away from Cast Heal Ball.

The ball spins and stares me down. "Get a job," Cast Heal Ball says. I simply grin while eating. "You need the job more than me."
